## ⚠️ Posting Type: LCA (Labor Condition Application)

This posting has the hallmarks of a DOL Labor Condition Application filing — the mechanism US employers use to sponsor H-1B workers at a specific location and pay band:

- Duties are boilerplate and **repeated** ("Design, develop, and test software applications. Design, develop, and/or test software applications using company technology stack.")
- **"Rate of Pay" prominently stated** — required by DOL
- **"May telecommute"** — standard DOL hedge
- No team name, no product area, no engineering org context
- Requirements list is a broad skills catch-all, not a specific team's stack

**What this means:**
- Real posting at a real company — Apply button is live, Uber is public and actively hiring
- **Strong F-1/H-1B signal** — Uber is actively filing LCAs = they intend to sponsor. This is not a promise, but it's the strongest structural evidence available
- **Unknown team** — applying through this posting enters a general SF SWE pool; Uber will match to a team. You cannot tailor the application to a specific product or engineering domain
- **Recommendation:** Search Uber's careers site for team-specific postings (search "software engineer new grad" or "university grad") — those give the team/product context needed for a targeted application. Use this LCA posting as a fallback or parallel track
